We are excited to host our 2026 Summer Rangers Academy Camp in partnership with Rangers Football Club.


This intensive training camp will be led by Rangers Academy coaches flying in directly from Scotland and features programs developed and delivered using the same style, philosophy, and mindset used within the Rangers Academy. Players will train for five days under a consistent curriculum focused on player development at a competitive level.



Camp Details
📍 Location: Clackamas High School Stadium
📅 Dates: July 27–July 31st
👕 Nike camp jersey included

Session Times & Cost by Birth Year
Age Group Time Cost
2nd to 4th Grade 9:00am - 12:00pm $250
5th to 10th Grade 1:00pm - 4:00pm $275

New this year - we are adding a specialized Goalkeeper Training Session, designed to provide focused, position-specific development in a small group setting.

More Information

This camp is intended for competitive players. Players from outside of Clackamas United are invited to join.


Older Players: If you are an 11th or 12th Grade player and are interested in participating, please contact our registrar using the information at the bottom of this page.


Registration & Waitlist Spots are limited. A waitlist is available, and additional capacity may be added if there is enough interest. If the camp is full, we encourage you to join the waitlist.


We look forward to welcoming players for an exciting and high-level training experience this summer!
