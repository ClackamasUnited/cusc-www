June 29, 2026

CUSC joins the N1 National League

By Tony Stevenson



Clackamas United is excited to announce that we have been accepted into the N1 National League for 2026/27.


This is a major milestone for our club and a testament to the hard work of our players, coaches, families, and staff. Competing in the N1 League will provide an even higher level of competition and development opportunities while continuing to build on the strong foundation of our club.


Thank you to everyone who has helped make this possible. The future is bright for Clackamas United, and we can’t wait to get started!.
