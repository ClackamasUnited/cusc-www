Free Veterans Day Hoodie for Every Classic Player

By Tony Stevenson


Clackamas United is partnering with our title sponsor, Seabee Construction, to give every player in our Classic program a free hoodie this Veterans Day to honor all veterans who have served their country.


The hoodie comes in the camo pattern shown above, and features our club crest on the chest. On the right sleeve is a special thank-you message to all veterans, tastefully printed in a khaki color to match the crest.


On the left sleeve, players have the option to add the seal of one of the six branches of the U.S. Military:

Air Force

Army

Coast Guard

Marine Corps

Navy

Space Force

The seal of the branch you select will be printed on the left sleeve in the same khaki color as the club crest.


Thank you to Seabee Construction for making this tribute to our veterans possible, and thank you to all who have served.
