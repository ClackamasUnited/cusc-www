About Winter Academy

Winter Academy is a great opportunity for players of all skill levels to keep improving during the off-season. Players will train with experienced coaches and focus on skill development and getting as many touches as possible each session.


Each session will include 4–5 rotating skill stations covering areas such as:

  • First Touch
  • Passing
  • Shooting
  • Technical Skills

Each night will have a different theme designed to help players become more complete and confident.


Winter Academy FAQ

Do I need to attend every session?

No! Attend as many sessions as your schedule allows. This flexible program is great for players balancing other sports and activities.

Do we play in all weather?

Yes, unless the temperature drops below 32°F or there is snow or lightning.

If a session is canceled, do we receive a refund?

No. Refunds are not issued for sessions canceled due to weather or facility conditions.

What should my player wear and bring?

  • Dress in layers and label all items.
  • Shin guards are required.
  • Bring a water bottle.
  • Bring an appropriately sized soccer ball with your name on it.

Can my child train with friends?

We’ll do our best to group friends together while making sure each player is in an environment that supports development and fun.

Can siblings be in the same group?

Groups are formed based on age and skill level.
