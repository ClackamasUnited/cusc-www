Celebrating Referees - October 11th is National Referees Day!

By Tony Stevenson


This October 11th we celebrate all of our referees, who dedicate their time, energy and love for the game week in, week out.


From helping players learn and grow, to keeping every match on track, referees play an essential role in creating a positive, fair, and safe environment for everyone on the field.


Clackamas United really appreciates all of the officials who show up for our players and teams, and enable them to play the beautiful game.


Thank you to all of our referees, and to referees all across the world!
