Celebrating Our Littles

By Tony Stevenson

Some of the biggest smiles at Clackamas United belong to our smallest players — the Littles!


Our Littles are the youngest members of our Recreational program, and for many of them this is their very first time on a soccer field. Every week they show up ready to run, kick, laugh, and have fun with their teammates.


At this age it's all about falling in love with the game: learning to dribble, chasing the ball as a pack, celebrating every goal like it's the World Cup final, and making new friends along the way.


A huge thank you to the volunteer coaches and the parents who make it all happen, week in and week out. None of this would be possible without you.
